Return Policy: Customers can return products within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Shipping: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free for orders above Rs. 999. Express shipping takes 1-2 days and costs Rs. 149.

Order Cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are shipped. Once shipped, the customer must use the return process.

Payment Methods: We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available for orders up to Rs. 5,000.

Warranty: Electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Warranty claims need the original invoice.

Customer Support Hours: Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST, at support@shopease.com or 1800-123-4567.
